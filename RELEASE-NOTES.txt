Battery Monitor v1.0.0 — Domovoi Assistant

- Night Workshop redesign with branded light and dark themes.
- Interactive Domovoi mascot: drag physics, individual animations, clickable community signs.
- Battery alerts at 20%, 10% and 5%, event history, device navigation, quick actions and optional desktop notifications.
- Approximate runtime estimates based on sufficient valid readings within the current session.
- Russian, Ukrainian and English; standalone taskbar widget or optional Windhawk integration.
- No telemetry; unknown readings are not replaced with guessed percentages.
- Bluetooth headsets and controllers remain experimental and device-dependent. AJAZZ AJ179 APEX USB receiver/dock was tested in earlier builds; Logitech G435 USB receiver does not provide automatic percentage.
